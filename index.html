Mission 3 • 7 minutes

🔢 Meet the IPv4 address

An IP address helps identify a device on a network. This is an example of an IPv4 address:

192.168.1.10
Remember: IPv4 uses 4 number groups, 3 dots, and each number is from 0 to 255.
1. How many number groups are in an IPv4 address?
2. How many dots separate the groups?
3. What is the largest possible number in one group?

Mission 5 • 7 minutes

💻 Find your IP address on a Microsoft Surface

Teacher demonstration: This activity is for a Windows computer such as a Microsoft Surface. If Command Prompt is blocked, use Windows Settings instead.
  1. Press Windows + R.
  2. Type cmd and press Enter.
  3. Type ipconfig and press Enter.
  4. Find IPv4 Address under the connected Wi-Fi or Ethernet adapter.
Example only — your screen may look different:
Wireless LAN adapter Wi-Fi:
   IPv4 Address . . . . . . . . . : 192.168.1.25
   Subnet Mask  . . . . . . . . . : 255.255.255.0
   Default Gateway . . . . . . . : 192.168.1.1
